1. **IE-27 problem statement**, OpenProblemsInNLA repository.
   - Website: https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/tree/main/linear-systems-and-elimination/IE-27
   - Raw page actually read: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/main/linear-systems-and-elimination/IE-27/README.md
   - The page specifies increasing Radau nodes, the unit-upper Crout convention, a Euclidean norm radius, symmetric positive-definite spatial matrices, and real positive shifts. Its status check is dated 11 September 2026.

2. **M. Outrata**, *On the recent advances of spectral analysis for systems arising from fully-implicit RK methods*, arXiv:2510.21241v1.
   - Primary-source HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2510.21241v1
   - Sections 2.1–2.3 distinguish the recalled general-stage conjecture from the polynomial/small-matrix equivalence. That equivalence is not itself the requested arbitrary-stage disk proof.

3. **I. Dravins, S. Serra-Capizzano, M. Neytcheva**, *Fine spectral analysis of preconditioned matrices and matrix sequences arising from stage-parallel implicit Runge–Kutta methods of arbitrarily high order*.
   - Primary-source HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2302.04657v2
   - Consulted for low-stage spectral analysis and the general symbolic reduction; no arbitrary-stage proof of the particular norm radius was extracted.

4. **O. Axelsson, I. Dravins, M. Neytcheva**, *Stage-parallel preconditioners for implicit Runge–Kutta methods of arbitrarily high order, linear problems*, Numerical Linear Algebra with Applications 31(1), e2532 (2024), DOI 10.1002/nla.2532.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/nla.2532
   - Original conjecture as identified by the problem page and retained report; not represented as a newly read complete paper in this continuation.
