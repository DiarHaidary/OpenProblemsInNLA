# Sources and attribution

Public sources were accessed on 13 September 2026 through the website, not a GitHub connector.

1. **OpenProblemsInNLA, SP-09.** The exact finite-normal amplification question and its recorded partial status.
   https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/tree/main/eigenvalues-and-inverse-problems/SP-09
   Raw statement: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/main/eigenvalues-and-inverse-problems/SP-09/README.md

2. **Sidney Holden, “SP-09 / SP-07: Exact orbit distance when one spectrum has two points,” 12 September 2026.** The prior supplied result for one spectrum with at most two distinct points. This package credits that theorem; it does not present it as new.
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/main/references/holden-spectral-2026-09-12/SP-09/proof.md

3. **Laurent W. Marcoux, Pawel Sarkowicz, and Yuanhang Zhang, “Kaplansky’s problem and unitary orbits in matrix amplifications,” arXiv:2508.13834.** Background on matrix amplification, self-adjoint special cases, and the distinction between general matrices and normal matrices. Results about zero orbit distance or arbitrary nonnormal pairs are not used as a solution of the finite positive-distance normal problem.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.13834

4. **Rajendra Bhatia, “Spectral Variation, Normal Matrices, and Finsler Geometry,” ISI preprint isid/ms/2007/03, 16 March 2007.** Primary mathematical exposition of spectral variation, short normal paths, unitary matrices, and concentric-circle/parallel-line cases. Its displayed equations and theorem statements were read from rendered PDF pages.
   https://repository.ias.ac.in/2530/1/318.pdf

5. **Qiyue Tang, “A Truncated Singular-Value Bound for Spectral Variation of Normal Matrices,” arXiv:2609.09177.** Hall-subspace/singular-value methods and the classical Krause three-by-three data. The displayed Krause norm calculation is an upper-bound witness for orbit distance; it is not treated as an already established lower bound. The all-amplifications lower bound in this package is supplied by the exact certificate.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09177

6. **OpenProblemsInNLA, CONTRIBUTING.md.** Repository distinction between partial and full resolutions and proof-review expectations.
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/main/CONTRIBUTING.md

The geometric special cases are standard-looking consequences of spectral-variation arguments; no claim of priority is made. The exact Krause lower-bound and rigidity certificate is a result established within this package. A comprehensive prior-art determination and independent peer review have not been completed.
