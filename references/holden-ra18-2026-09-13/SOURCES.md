# Source inventory

Public sources were accessed through their websites, not through a GitHub plugin. Access date: September 13, 2026. No third-party paper is redistributed in this archive.

## 1. Canonical problem statement

OpenProblemsInNLA, **RA-18 — The Goreinov–Tyrtyshnikov–Zamarashkin conjecture on square-submatrix inverse norms**. Status check dated September 11, 2026.

- Rendered entry: https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/blob/main/randomized-and-low-rank-approximation/RA-18/README.md
- Raw entry: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/main/randomized-and-low-rank-approximation/RA-18/README.md

Used for the precise real conjecture, the separately proposed universal complex extension, the recorded status, and bibliographic context. The proposed complex constant is explicitly required to be independent of both `r` and `n`.

## 2. Original pseudoskeleton source

S. A. Goreinov, E. E. Tyrtyshnikov, and N. L. Zamarashkin, **A theory of pseudoskeleton approximations**, Linear Algebra and its Applications 261 (1997), 1–21.

https://doi.org/10.1016/S0024-3795(96)00301-1

Bibliographic and historical attribution follows the canonical RA-18 entry; the original publisher text was not used as an unseen source of a proof. The manuscript includes its own short proof of the classical maximal-volume bound.

## 3. Real two-column result

Richik Sengupta and Mikhail Pautov, **On the submatrices with the best-bounded inverses**, arXiv:2604.05944v5, April 16, 2026.

https://arxiv.org/html/2604.05944v5

The primary HTML text was read. Its real rank-two theorem is the external input in the structured real result. It is not an input to the complex counterexample theorem. The version is a preprint; no stronger review status is claimed.

## 4. Complex two-column upper bound

Yuri Nesterenko, **Submatrices with the best-bounded inverses: an asymptotically tight upper bound for C^(n x 2)**, arXiv:2604.24087v1, April 27, 2026.

https://arxiv.org/html/2604.24087v1

The primary HTML text was read. Proposition 1 gives the constant `(2-2/sqrt(3))^(-1/2)` and the equality condition. Used to distinguish the known fixed-two-column phenomenon from the growing-rank obstruction proved here, and as an input for the analogous restricted complex row-direction class.

## 5. Earlier complex counterexample

Yuri Nesterenko, **Submatrices with the best-bounded inverses: Studying R^(n x 2) and C^(n x 2)**, arXiv:2408.16631v1, August 29, 2024.

https://arxiv.org/html/2408.16631v1

The primary HTML text was read for the two-column complex obstruction and geometric context. The archive does not reproduce that paper's proof or figures.

## 6. Structured real extremal examples

Yuri Nesterenko, **About subspaces the most deviating from the coordinate ones**, arXiv:2511.02387v2, 2026 revision of a 2025 preprint.

https://arxiv.org/html/2511.02387v2

The primary text was inspected for its graph-based construction and weighting theorem. Cited as related work on structured real equality examples. No assertion is made that the unequal-duplication fixture or every auxiliary special case is absent from this literature.

## Further background consulted

Yuri Nesterenko, **Submatrices with the best-bounded inverses: revisiting the hypothesis**, arXiv:2303.07492, revised 2024.

A targeted literature search did not identify this exact recursive obstruction, but this is not a claim of an exhaustive novelty search or independent priority verification.
