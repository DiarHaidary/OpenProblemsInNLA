## External theorem used in the new proof

H. Bercovici, B. Collins, K. Dykema, W. S. Li, and D. Timotin, “Intersections of Schubert Varieties and Eigenvalue Inequalities in an Arbitrary Finite Factor,” arXiv:0805.4817v1, 30 May 2008, Theorem 0.2.

https://arxiv.org/html/0805.4817v1

The theorem produces finite-dimensional zero-sum Hermitian triples whose eigenvalues are consecutive block integrals of the original spectral functions. Multiplying all three output matrices by the target dimension turns these integrals into averages. Section 2 of the new manuscript then gives a partial-sum gluing argument for a finite list of Hermitian summands. This Hermitian consequence is classical input, not a newly claimed Horn theorem.

The source paper is linked, not redistributed in this archive.

Context only. Results for self-adjoint matrices, zero orbit distance, general C*-algebras, or nonnormal counterexamples are not treated as a solution of finite-normal SP-09.
