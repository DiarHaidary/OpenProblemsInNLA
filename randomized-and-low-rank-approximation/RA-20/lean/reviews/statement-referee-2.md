## Mathematical fidelity and adversarial scope checks

1. **The full target and field survive.** `criticalCountConjecture` quantifies over all `n≥3`, `1≤s≤4`, `s≤n`, and retains all four formulas. Under these ranges its natural-number subtractions agree with ordinary arithmetic. The negative case `n=s=3` is admissible and predicts four. Every matrix and critical-point coordinate is complex. `Matrix.IsSymm` is transpose symmetry, with genuine rank over the complex field; it is not Hermitian symmetry or a real-only restriction.

2. **The variety and reduced ring are genuine.** `variety` is the original rank-at-most-two constrained matrix set. `definingIdeal` uses all polynomials vanishing on that actual set; it is not a proposed determinant ideal inserted as a definition. Its quotient is therefore the reduced affine coordinate ring. The coordinate-preserving isomorphism with `C[a,b,c]/(abc)` is a separate contract covering all nine matrix coordinates. The proposed hypersurface is reduced because `abc` is square-free; that algebraic fact still has to be established in the implementation rather than assumed.

3. **Smoothness is not defined away.** `SmoothPoint` first requires actual variety membership and then the actual point prime in the quotient, identified by pullback to the evaluation prime. It uses Mathlib's `Algebra.smoothLocus` over `C`. The pinned definition is formal smoothness of the localized ring; the library's finite-presentation localization theorems give the ordinary geometric notion at a point. There is no chosen chart, assumed dimension, Jacobian rank, rank-two-stratum convention or `ExactlyOneZero` premise hidden in this definition. The contract deriving exactly one zero coordinate is correct for the reduced union of the three planes and excludes every plane intersection, including axis points of matrix rank two. The quotient representation and this genuine smoothness equivalence remain substantial unproved obligations.

4. **The entire tangent ideal is retained.** `TangentVector` annihilates polynomial differentials for every element of the reduced vanishing ideal in all nine original coordinates. Its proposed equivalent equations impose symmetry, the three zero diagonal tangent entries, and `bc Z01 + ac Z02 + ab Z12=0`. The equivalence is explicitly required at singular as well as smooth points. It cannot be replaced by assuming the determinant differential is the complete tangent condition without proving the ideal bridge.

5. **The distance and derivative are the original ones.** `fullFrobeniusDistance` sums all nine entry squares without conjugation. On symmetric matrices this gives weight two off the diagonal, and the complete restricted-distance identity is an explicit obligation. `SmoothCriticalPoint` applies the actual complex `fderiv` to every actual tangent direction. The pinned derivative is defined for topological vector spaces, and the matrix instances are the ordinary Hausdorff finite product topology with continuous addition/scalar action; no choice of a matrix norm changes the objective. Its zero default outside differentiability cannot justify a shortcut: the polynomial objective and its full derivative formula are fixed and must be proved. The component contract similarly requires the actual second derivative.

6. **Genericity cannot be made vacuous.** `HasGenericCriticalCount` quantifies over an arbitrary polynomial and requires an actual symmetric matrix where it is nonzero before asserting the count on that principal open set. This is equivalent to holding outside a proper algebraic exceptional set in the symmetric affine data space. The chosen product of the three off-diagonal data coordinates has a nonempty open complement, but the draft does not substitute that particular open into an unknown competing generic-count claim. `generic_data_intersection` is an explicit contract. Independently, its mathematical route is sound: restrict the unknown polynomial to the six free symmetric coordinates; its nonzero value prevents it being the zero polynomial. The product with the nonzero off-diagonal monomial is nonzero in the polynomial domain over the infinite field `C`, so some symmetric point avoids both zero sets. Diagonal data remain arbitrary.

7. **Counting does not silently lose infinite sets or components.** The count is `Cardinal.mk` of the actual smooth critical-set subtype, equal to a natural cardinal; it is not `Nat.card` assigning zero to an infinite type. Exhaustion and injectivity of the three actual candidate matrices are required for every data matrix in the specified generic set. For nonzero data coordinates each candidate is smooth, the three are distinct, and every smooth point belongs to exactly one component. The generic-intersection contract is then sufficient to rule out a different generic count four and negate the complete original conjunction.

8. **Multiplicity scope is honest.** The canonical target counts distinct smooth critical points for generic data. The extra Hessian contract correctly states the actual bilinear Hessian `4 sum h_i v_i` and zero kernel for all three charts, even for nonsymmetric data in that supporting theorem. It supports nondegeneracy. The package does not export a separate scheme-theoretic intersection-multiplicity theorem; future descriptions should not claim one. Such an additional theorem is not needed for the retained canonical cardinal-count target.
